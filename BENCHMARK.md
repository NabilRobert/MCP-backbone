### Notes on run 1

Both failures were investigated, not just counted:

- **"What is the total of all payment amounts?" (easy): a real failure,
  partly caused by the tool.** `payment` is a partitioned table, so
  querying it already includes all 55 monthly partitions. But
  `list_tables` lists each partition as a table of its own, and the agent
  summed the parent *and* every partition, counting each payment twice.
  Since fixed: `list_tables` now shows a partitioned table once and hides
  its partitions (Pagila lists 15 tables instead of 70). This run predates
  the fix.
- **"Which film category has generated the most payment revenue?" (hard):
  a defect in the question, not the agent.** In this Pagila variant 964 of
  1,000 films belong to more than one category, so "revenue per category"
  has two defensible readings. The answer key counts a film's full revenue
  toward each of its categories; the agent split it across them. The
  question now says which reading it means. This run is reported as it was
  graded, not re-scored.

The strict and lenient scores came out identical: every correct answer was
exactly right, with no rounding or extra columns to forgive.
